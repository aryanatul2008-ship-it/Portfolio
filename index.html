Witness

A privacy-first tool that lets activists, journalists and investigators prove a piece of evidence existed at a specific time and has not been changed since.

  • Encrypts and hashes the file on the user's device, so the server never sees it
  • Stores the encrypted file on IPFS and anchors its hash on a blockchain
  • Works offline and syncs later, with an optional AI check for text

Bridges the gap between learning and hiring. It gives students a custom AI study roadmap, a live coding arena with a no-spoilers AI mentor, and job matching based on the skills they have proven.
